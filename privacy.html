Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 21, 2026

OpenAd is built to help you use your phone more intentionally, so privacy is central to how it works. The app has no account and no server of ours — your Screen Time and app-selection data stay on your device. The app shows rewarded ads through third-party advertising partners, which collect limited device data to serve them (details below).

This Privacy Policy explains what information the OpenAd app (the "App", "we", "us") handles and the choices you have. The App is operated by Seven Agent Studios LLC ("the developer"). By using the App you agree to this Policy and to our Terms of Service.

1. Summary

  • No account required. The App works without registration or a login. We may offer optional account features in the future (see §6).
  • Your data stays on your device. Your gated-app choices, usage activity, and stats are stored only on your phone.
  • Screen Time data is never transmitted to us or to advertisers.
  • Ads are served by third-party ad partners, which collect limited device data to show rewarded ads.
  • We use privacy-respecting product analytics (PostHog) to count app opens and feature usage. It never receives your Screen Time data and isn't used to track you across other apps.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

2. Information we handle

a. Screen Time & app-selection data (on-device only)

The App uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks. The apps, categories, and websites you choose to gate — and the on-device activity that triggers a shield — are represented by opaque tokens Apple keeps on your device. This is stored only in a private, on-device App Group container. It is never sent to our servers (we have none) and is never shared with advertisers or any third party, consistent with Apple's App Store Review Guideline 5.1.2.

b. Usage stats (on-device only)

The App keeps simple counters — such as the number of unlocks and ads watched — to show you on the dashboard. These never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

c. Advertising data

Rewarded ads are delivered by third-party advertising partners. OpenAd currently uses Unity LevelPlay to request rewarded ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange in the updated app. Earlier versions may use Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) or Google AdMob. The advertising provider depends on the version installed. To serve and measure ads, an ad partner's SDK may collect limited information such as device identifiers, IP address, coarse location, and ad-interaction data, as described in that partner's privacy policy. We never provide ad partners with your Screen Time or app-selection data.

The Unity LevelPlay version requests restricted advertising processing and does not treat Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission as consent to personalized advertising. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad partners may still process data to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Ads also use Apple's SKAdNetwork attribution.

d. Diagnostics & analytics

To understand how the App is used and to fix bugs, OpenAd uses PostHog product analytics. PostHog records in-app events — such as app opens, screens viewed, and actions you take (for example, watching an ad or enabling reminders) — together with an app-generated anonymous identifier and basic device and app information (such as device model, OS version, and app version). This data is not linked to your real-world identity, is not used to track you across other apps or websites, and never includes your Screen Time or app-selection data. We use it only to improve the App. PostHog processes this data on our behalf; see posthog.com/privacy.

4. Data retention & deletion

Deleting the App removes its local data. Advertising and analytics data already processed by third-party providers is subject to their retention policies; deleting the App does not automatically delete those records. For questions or deletion requests, contact us at the address below or use the provider's privacy controls.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Future features & accounts

OpenAd may evolve over time. We may, in the future, introduce optional features such as accounts or syncing your stats across devices, additional advertising or analytics partners, or other capabilities. If and when we do, we will update this Policy to describe how any new data is handled before those features collect it.

For questions or requests relating to OpenAd or its service providers, contact us below.
